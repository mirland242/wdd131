Color Schema

  • Headings (Burgundy): #800020
  • Body Text (black): #333333
  • page background (slight white): rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)
  • Buttons (dark yellow): #ffca3a
  • footer (Dark Burgundy): #5C0013

Typography

Merriweather will be used for headings and page titles. Its professional and elegant appearance helps emphasize important content.

Open Sans will be used for body text, navigation menus, and other general content because it is clean, modern, and highly readable.
